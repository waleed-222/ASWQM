# ASWQM: Autonomous Sail Boat for Water Quality Monitoring

## Overview
ASWQM (Autonomous Sail Boat for Water Quality Monitoring) is a remotely operated system designed to monitor water conditions in real-time. It provides a safe and cost-effective solution for assessing water quality in remote or hazardous environments.

## Objectives
- Provide **accurate and real-time data** on water quality conditions.
- Monitor **remote and hard-to-access locations** without requiring manual sampling.
- Reduce **costs** by eliminating frequent human sampling and lab tests.
- **Classify water quality** automatically as "drinkable" or "polluted" based on sensor readings and predefined thresholds.

## Abstract
Traditional water monitoring methods rely on **manual sampling**, which can be hazardous, costly, and time-consuming. ASWQM addresses these challenges by using a **remotely operated autonomous boat** equipped with sensors. The system utilizes a **wireless communication protocol** to keep workers at a safe distance while continuously gathering water quality data.

## Background
Manual water quality monitoring involves physical data collection and laboratory testing, making it inefficient for remote areas. **ASWQM automates the process**, providing continuous monitoring without requiring human intervention. The system integrates a **mobile application** for real-time control and data visualization.

## Methods
### **Water Sensors**
- **pH Sensor**: Measures acidity levels.
- **Turbidity Sensor**: Detects water clarity.
- **TDS Sensor**: Measures dissolved particles.
- **Water Temperature Sensor**: Tracks temperature variations.

### **Mobile Application**
- Interface for **controlling the boat**.
- Displays **real-time sensor data**.

### **Navigation & Motion**
- Users send a **target location** via the mobile app.
- The **MPU (Motion Processing Unit)** adjusts direction automatically.
- The boat successfully navigates to the desired location and starts monitoring.

## Results
### **Water Quality Measurements**
- **pH Range:** 6.5 - 8.5
- **Turbidity:** 0 - 120 NTU
- **Dissolved Particles:** 0 - 20 mg/L
- **Temperature:** 20 - 30°C

During real-world testing, the system detected:
- A rapid **rise in turbidity (10 → 90 NTU)** after a rainstorm.
- A **drop in pH (7.8 → 6.8)** due to contamination.

### **Effectiveness of Mobile Application**
- Allowed **intuitive control** of the boat and sensor data monitoring.
- Personnel required **minimal training** to operate the system.

### **Navigation Accuracy**
- The boat successfully reached target locations **within 5 meters** on **90% of missions**.
- Improved **MPU calibration** reduced navigation errors.

## Conclusion
ASWQM provides a **safe, autonomous, and efficient** method for monitoring water quality. By integrating **sensor technology, wireless communication, and mobile applications**, it ensures reliable real-time data collection while reducing operational risks and costs.
